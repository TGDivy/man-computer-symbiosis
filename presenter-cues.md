# Presenter Cues

Short operating copy for the implemented 32-scene deck. Use `speaker-notes.md` for full language and sourcing.

## Room preflight

- Use a wide landscape display at 100% browser zoom. Verify both 1600×900 and 1366×768 if the room setup is uncertain; the mobile/portrait gate is intentional.
- Load every local image, enter fullscreen, and test **Space**, **ArrowLeft**, **N**, **R**, **Escape**, and **M**. The first presentation gesture arms browser audio.
- Sound-check with speech at room level. Confirm the Scene 03 and 13 vacuums, Scene 12 pressure, Scene 19 break, Scene 29 subtraction, and Scene 30 resolution. Automated checks validate mappings, not perceived loudness.
- Decide before the talk whether reduced motion will be enabled. If it is, Scene 12 must be stepped manually through Builds 1–5.
- Preflight Scene 24 in the exact managed browser, account, and network. Browser- or vendor-operated recognition may process audio; the page itself does not retain it. Keep **Use text instead** as the immediate fallback.
- Coordinate Scene 24 with the CART or live-caption operator. Tell them that the on-slide transcript is part of the demo, not the official caption channel, and agree whether synthetic playback should be used.
- Do not depend on Scene 18 or 27 for data. Both are temporary room discussions; nothing is exported, persisted, or tallied.

## Recovery cues

- If sound fails, continue silently; the argument does not depend on audio. Do not troubleshoot in front of the room.
- If Scene 24 fails, select **Use text instead**, enter the phrase, and continue to playback only if it is ready.
- If an audience interaction stalls, offer one prepared example, accept it as the room's first response, and move on.
- If time contracts, preserve the transitions into Scenes 03, 13, 19, 29, and 30; they carry the structural rhythm.
